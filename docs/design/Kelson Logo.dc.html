Turn 1 · logo exploration

kelson

Four marks in the CNCF register — flat, single-weight geometry that survives at 16px and reads as a fabrication drawing. Each refers to the kelson: the beam laid over a ship's keel that binds the frames into one hull.

1A Hull section
kelson

Cross-section of a hull with the keel and its kelson below. Most literal of the four; the descender doubles as a plumb line.

1B Bound frames
kelson

Hexagonal container in the container-ecosystem idiom, with the spine and two frames it ties together. Closest sibling to existing CNCF marks.

1C Laid keel
kelson

Three stacked timbers narrowing to the keel's V. Reads as stacked layers — platform on cluster — and as a hull in profile.

1D Scarf joint
kelson

Diamond crossed by two beams of unequal length — the offset of a scarf joint. Quietest and most abstract; scales furthest down.
